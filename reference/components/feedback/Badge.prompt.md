Small pill badge for status and categories — sync state, draft/published, severity.

```jsx
<Badge tone="success" dot>Synced</Badge>
<Badge tone="warning" dot>Offline</Badge>
<Badge tone="brand">EGRA</Badge>
<Badge tone="error" solid>3 errors</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral` `brand` `accent` `success` `warning` `error` `info`. Flags: `dot`, `solid`.
